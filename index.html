Research

My research mainly focuses on reinforcement learning — spanning deep reinforcement learning, inverse reinforcement learning, and hierarchical approaches — applied to vision-based navigation and control for drones and robotic manipulators. More recently, I have been working on integrating and fine-tuning Vision-Language-Action models for real-world robot manipulation tasks.

Stable Online Adaptation of Diffusion Policies via Latent-Regularized Steering and Periodic Resetting Under Preparation
Minwoo Kim, Taehan Kim, Wonhyuk Choi, Jihwan Kim
Samsung Research
We propose LRDS, an online RL framework for stable fine-tuning of pretrained diffusion policies in long-horizon, high-precision manipulation. LRDS introduces Gaussian-space regularization with DCT-based noise parameterization, a periodic resetting mechanism, and a distributional critic for bimodal value landscapes. Evaluated on a 44-DoF real-world robot with a fine-tuned π0.5 policy, LRDS raises task success from 40% to 90% (18/20 trials) within one hour of online training.

Job Experience

Staff Engineer, Samsung Research (Robot Intelligence Team) | 04/2025 - Present

Research centers on sample-efficient online reinforcement learning for real-world robot manipulation and on integrating RL with Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models — algorithms that reach deployable performance within short real-world interaction budgets. Led planning and execution of VLA+RL projects end to end: simulation feasibility, real-world deployment, data acquisition, and pipeline design.

Online RL for Precision Manipulation (Human-in-the-Loop)

  • Built human-intervention-based online RL pipelines for contact-rich, tight-tolerance insertion on real hardware, integrating offline RL pretraining, warm-start RL, and human intervention to compress the online interaction budget.
  • Cable insertion (multi peg-in-hole) — 100% success (20/20) within 40 minutes of online training; evaluated post-training under active physical disturbance, with an operator pushing and displacing the arm mid-execution.
  • Water hose insertion (elastic, deformable object) — same methodology, 100% success (20/20) within 40 minutes of online training.

VLA + RL Integration

  • LRDS (Latent-Regularized Diffusion Steering) — RL-guided selection of the initial diffusion noise in a VLA action head, steering sampling toward high-value actions;
  • Residual RL — an RL policy learns a corrective residual on top of VLA action output.
  • Anchored action generation — RL generates actions constrained to remain anchored to the VLA action prior.
  • Online dataset aggregation — improving pretrained VLA performance through online rollout with human intervention, aggregating on-policy corrective data on real hardware.

Tactile-Driven Dexterous Manipulation

  • Zero-shot sim-to-real transfer of tactile-conditioned RL policies to real hardware, enabling tasks infeasible under conventional teleoperation (e.g., bottle-cap opening).
  • RMA (Rapid Motor Adaptation)-based in-hand object rotation policy trained across diverse objects for robustness under object-property variation.
  • Extensive reward function design and ablation for tactile-conditioned policy learning.
  • Developing a unified VLA architecture that ingests tactile sensing.

VLA Model Development

  • Building manipulation VLA models on π-series (π0.5) and GR00T bases: VLM backbone augmented with separate action experts specialized for arm and high-DoF hand control.
  • Target platform: Rainbow Robotics RBY-1 + Sharpa Hand (21 DoF), specialized for dexterous tasks such as card swiping and sticky-note picking.
  • Built foundation models for unstructured precision manufacturing tasks using large-scale demonstration data.

Platforms & Tooling

  • Robots: Rainbow Robotics RBY-1, Franka, 44-DoF humanoid. Hands: Sharpa Hand (21 DoF), RobotEra XHand.
  • Simulation: Isaac Gym / Isaac Lab, humanoid manipulation benchmark suites, LIBERO, robosuite (DexMimicGen).
